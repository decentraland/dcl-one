# Publish

Publishing a scene for mobile follows the **same process as any other scene** — deploy via the Creator Hub or the CLI as described in the [Publishing](/docs/creator/sdk7/publishing/publishing/) guide. The pages in this section cover what’s specific to mobile on top of that standard flow.


> [!NOTE]
> If you haven’t published your scene yet, start with [Publishing](/docs/creator/sdk7/publishing/publishing/) under Scenes (SDK7). The steps below apply once your scene is already live.


- [Get featured](/docs/creator/build-for-mobile/publish/get-featured/): How to get your scene featured in the mobile Discover section.
- [iOS curation](/docs/creator/build-for-mobile/publish/ios-curation/): How iOS content curation works and how to submit your scene or world for review.
