## Events scheduled jobs

[Server registration](https://github.com/decentraland/events/blob/770e43f8baaf47844bf4ff5768e5493a380508bc/src/server.ts#L37) runs three notification jobs and recurrence every minute; foundation addresses refresh every five minutes.

| Job | Writes/effects | Rehearsal implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `updateNextStartAt` | [Recurrence expansion](https://github.com/decentraland/events/blob/770e43f8baaf47844bf4ff5768e5493a380508bc/src/entities/Event/cron.ts#L23) updates `events` recurrence dates/next occurrence. | Not implemented in the retained pilot. Future Events coverage must execute this real job against fixtures and verify its committed row changes; target recurrence stays off. |
| Upcoming, started and ended notifications | [Notification jobs](https://github.com/decentraland/events/blob/770e43f8baaf47844bf4ff5768e5493a380508bc/src/entities/Event/cron.ts#L51) publish SNS, upcoming Slack, and advance `notification_cursors`; started events can fetch Social community members. | Copy event facts only. Cursor equality is not proof of delivery. Duplicate target execution could notify twice or alter Social ranking twice. |
| `refreshFoundationAddresses` | [Feature-flag refresh](https://github.com/decentraland/events/blob/770e43f8baaf47844bf4ff5768e5493a380508bc/src/modules/decentralandFoundationAddresses.ts#L31) updates process-local cache. | Not DB replication; public read equivalence also depends on configuration/cache behavior. |

**Source defect found:** recurrence calls `EventModel.update(...)` without awaiting it in [cron.ts](https://github.com/decentraland/events/blob/770e43f8baaf47844bf4ff5768e5493a380508bc/src/entities/Event/cron.ts#L47). The job can report completion before the write finishes and cannot catch that promise's rejection through its own lifecycle. A separate Events fix should await the write and verify failed-write propagation before using that job's completion as rehearsal evidence. A future capture adapter would observe successful commits; the defect concerns job lifecycle, not proof that schedules require DB sync.

## Social jobs and internal traffic

- [Daily community ranking](https://github.com/decentraland/social-service-ea/blob/143e9aa3c2fe21c9e44e9541e7c8dc2561a9d1ec/src/components.ts#L287) writes scores after reading communities/metrics; initial execution waits 30 minutes. Its outputs are not settings facts.
- [Private voice expiry and community polling](https://github.com/decentraland/social-service-ea/blob/143e9aa3c2fe21c9e44e9541e7c8dc2561a9d1ec/src/components.ts#L326) expire call rows and publish updates, or query Gatekeeper and update the voice cache. Neither should start in the follower.
- [SQS handlers](https://github.com/decentraland/social-service-ea/blob/143e9aa3c2fe21c9e44e9541e7c8dc2561a9d1ec/src/controllers/handlers/sqs/handler.ts) consume login, event-ended, photo and streaming messages. For example, [event-ended handling](https://github.com/decentraland/social-service-ea/blob/143e9aa3c2fe21c9e44e9541e7c8dc2561a9d1ec/src/controllers/handlers/sqs/event-ended-handler.ts) increments community metrics. Running another consumer/processor is not equivalent to safely duplicating its source outcome.
- [Peer synchronization](https://github.com/decentraland/social-service-ea/blob/143e9aa3c2fe21c9e44e9541e7c8dc2561a9d1ec/src/adapters/peers-synchronizer.ts) and [subscriber reconciliation](https://github.com/decentraland/social-service-ea/blob/143e9aa3c2fe21c9e44e9541e7c8dc2561a9d1ec/src/adapters/rpc-server/subscribers-context.ts) maintain Redis/presence and live subscriber state. They are separate from durable PostgreSQL synchronization.

## Ordered next work and repository changes

Use the [operation backlog](operation-classification.md). Schedule, RSVP, settings, mute and relationship commands need native compatibility assessment; do not add DB adapters simply because their keys look easy to map. Event recurrence and Social ranking/queue metrics are residual candidates only after safe replay of their inputs is assessed.

The Events missing-await fix has a job-lifecycle justification independent of synchronization. Source application changes may also be needed for durable acceptance receipts or effect suppression. Any residual DB installation needs exact writer provenance and mixed-transaction handling. Neither duplicate requests nor row copying establishes provider/privacy or WebSocket delivery parity.

No deployed installations, load tests or live-job runs were performed for this audit.
